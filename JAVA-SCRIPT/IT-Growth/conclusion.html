FINAL ANSWER

Developers Are Not Simply Disappearing.

The role of the developer is changing. AI can automate parts of coding and development, but humans remain important for understanding problems, designing systems, validating results, managing risks and making decisions.

The developer of 2030 will likely be someone who combines strong software engineering fundamentals with AI fluency, system thinking, security awareness and human skills.

THE FUTURE STARTS NOW

Learn. Build. Adapt. Evolve.

Technology will continue changing. The strongest developers will be the ones who continue learning and know how to use new tools effectively.
