01 — TECHNOLOGY

Technology Driving Change

Several technologies are reshaping the IT industry and changing how software is designed, built and operated.

KEY TECHNOLOGIES

The Technologies Shaping IT 2030

🤖

Generative AI

Generative AI can create text, code, documentation, designs and other digital content from natural-language instructions.

⚡

AI Automation

AI can automate repetitive workflows, analysis, support operations and parts of software development.

🧠

AI Agents

AI agents can perform sequences of tasks, use tools, make decisions within defined boundaries and interact with software systems.

☁️

Cloud Computing

Cloud platforms provide scalable infrastructure, databases, computing resources and services.

🔐

Cybersecurity

Security will become increasingly important as more systems, applications and AI services become connected.

📊

Data & APIs

Data platforms and APIs connect applications, services and AI systems across digital environments.

WHY IT MATTERS

Technology Does Not Work Alone

The important change is not one technology by itself. The bigger transformation happens when AI, cloud, data, automation and cybersecurity work together.
